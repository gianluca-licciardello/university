1. Describe the problem the app is trying to solve in max 3 sentences. What problem does the app solve, how (solution), and for whom (user)?


The workflow of students and academic researchers requires them to constantly acquire, process, and create complex information scattered across highly heterogeneous landscapes. The app aims at enriching documents with metadata and extracting topics to facilitate organization of the information, its retrieval and interconnection between topics from different areas.
